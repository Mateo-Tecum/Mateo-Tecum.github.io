## Multimaterial Pliers

I designed and fabricated pliers that were 3d printed using two different filament types: TPU and PLA. The design was made in Fusion, with the materials having interlocking features so that the two materials could link together with ease.

My portion included making the assembly and parts and engineering drawings of the entire CAD model. It also included created the 3d printed custom parts.
