## Overview

I designed and built a four-legged robotic platform using SolidWorks, Arduino, servo motors, and 3D-printed components.

The project combined mechanical design, fabrication, electronics, and embedded programming.

## Mechanical Design

The robot's components were modeled in SolidWorks before fabrication. The design focused on creating a lightweight structure capable of supporting the servo-driven leg mechanisms.

## Electronics

An Arduino was used to control the servo motors responsible for the robot's leg movement.

## Fabrication

Structural components were manufactured using 3D printing and assembled with the motors and electronics.
