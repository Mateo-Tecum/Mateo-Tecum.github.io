How ePFF works:
![PFF concept](/assets/img/probe_holder_concept.png)

Task Specifications: 
•	Must fit both probe sizes.
o	Please see the following Fusion folder: CHBE 4200 -> Fall 2026 -> Ultrasound-Project-Shared.
•	The probe should sit close to Ellie’s palm. Ellie has significant wrist mobility. One degree of freedom may help the probe follow the patient’s arm.
•	Chemically compatible with germicidal wipes.
•	Use a material and finish suitable for the intended skin contact. If you use Tough 1500 Resin, follow the manufacturer’s post-processing instructions for the version you print.
•	The probe holder needs to improve on Ellie’s current solution. Ellie uses tape, which has two problems.
o	The probe shifts over time and when Ellie applies pressure.
•	Model at least two custom holder components in context. Use the arm scan, probe models, or shared sketches to drive their geometry. In a hybrid design, you can model the other components separately.
o	Tape looks makeshift. Ellie joked about having to tell patients that she knew what she was doing. The holder should look professional.
